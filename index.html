// 01 · ABOUT

background & approach.

I build full-stack web applications and developer tools with TypeScript, Next.js, Node.js, and PostgreSQL.

I hold a Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) in Computer Science and Engineering from Visvesvaraya Technological University (Acharya Institute of Technology), Bengaluru.

I care about shipping software that feels fast, works reliably, and is straightforward to maintain. Rather than chasing every new framework, I focus on solid fundamentals: clean database schemas, predictable state, and accessible interfaces.

Lately, I have been building interactive systems and voice-driven software—such as Outpost, a real-time collaborative workspace that combines an infinite canvas with AI document editing, and Bolo India, a voice-first platform tailored for Indian multilingual workflows.

When I am not writing code, I read technical documentation, explore open-source software architectures, and experiment with developer tooling.
